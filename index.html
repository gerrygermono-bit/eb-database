EB Database Guidelines

Welcome to the EB Database!

This guide covers everything — Plantilla schedules, commitments, the calendar of activities, and the links hub. Use the quick-jump below or scroll through.

Getting Started

  • Sign in with your official @dlsu.edu.ph Google account. Access is restricted to members listed in the EB roster.
  • If you're new, ask a Top 3 member to add you to the EB_Members roster first.
  • Toggle Light and Dark mode with the sun/moon icon in the header.
  • Use the panels to navigate: Overview, Plantilla, Calendar & Workshops, Links Hub, and EB Members.
  • Everything is saved to the EB Database sheet and refreshes automatically (about 30 seconds).

Plantilla — Availability

  • Click Edit My Availability to set your schedule on the weekly grid.
  • Each cell is a half-hour block from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Pick a color from the legend, then click or drag to paint: yellow = online class, orange = face-to-face class, pink = willing to go to campus, blue = personal matters. Pick the blank swatch to erase.
  • Mark your campus-available time pink — that's what Find a Free Time counts as free. Use the per-day all / clear shortcuts to fill or erase whole days.
  • Use the Conflicts field for repeating schedule notes (classes, org commitments).
  • The grid is per term — switch terms with the dropdown above the grid.
  • Add Commitment for one-off dates that don't repeat, e.g. exams or events. Types: OJT, Practicum, Thesis, Org, Personal, Other.
  • You can edit your own availability and commitments. Top 3 can also edit them for you.

Find a Free Time

  • Great for scheduling meetings or shoots — pick any combination of members and days, set a minimum duration, then hit Find.
  • Results show the overlapping free blocks for the selected group, plus how many people are free in each block.
  • Tap a member or day chip to include or exclude them from the search.

Calendar & Workshops

  • The month view shows all activities — workshops, ARW, SMOOGA, LMW, Lakan, General Assembly, orgwide events, and meetings.
  • Each category has its own color; use the legend chips to focus on specific categories.
  • Set the activity status: Planned, Confirmed, Ongoing, Completed, or Cancelled.
  • A banner warns you when activities on the same day overlap in time.
  • Click any day to open the day drawer with that day's full schedule.
  • Any EB member can add, edit, or delete activities with + New Activity.

Links Hub

  • One place for all pertinent files — templates, policies & TOR, sourcing & speaker forms, ARW materials, and Google Drive files.
  • Essential links (PPF Template, TOR, SPCA) are pinned to the top with an "essential" tag.
  • Use the search box and category chips to filter, and + Add Link to contribute.
  • The Order field controls sorting; uncheck Active to hide a link from the hub without deleting it.

EB Members & Roles

  • The EB Members panel lists the roster with positions and sections.
  • Being on the roster with Active = TRUE is what grants access to the database.
  • Top 3 only: add or remove members, toggle Top 3 / Active flags, and Set Term.
  • Top 3 can also edit any EB member's availability and commitments; EB members can only edit their own.
  • The last active Top 3 cannot be removed, so the team never loses admin access.

Feedback

  • Use the Feedback button (message icon) to send a suggestion, report a bug, or share general feedback.
  • Bug reports are logged in the Bugs tab of the sheet — include the feature and steps so it's easy to reproduce.
